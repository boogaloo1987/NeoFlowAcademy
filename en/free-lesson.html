Where does your weight go on the last beat?

A weight-and-support exercise to practise at home, in full: we watch the movement, break it down, try it, then choose an intention.

How it works

Four steps, one single instruction

The movement is shown in full, then broken down, then repeated in full. You only have one thing to remember and try.
