Dancer Training

NeoFlowAcademy

NeoFlowAcademy is dancer training led by Regis Tsoumbou bakana: classes, workshops and masterclasses in hip-hop and contemporary dance, dedicated to passing on and sharing the practice of movement.

“My vision of dance is to pass on what I know to as many people as possible, to share my experience, and to let everyone explore their own relationship with movement.”

NeoFlow Essentiels

Confidence Artistic expression Choreographic creation Professional career

For dancers who train regularly but feel they're missing a method, a direction, or the confidence to go further. NeoFlow Essentiels supports that shift: dancing with more confidence, understanding your movement more deeply, learning to express an intention, and starting to create your own choreographic material.

Free content (TikTok / Instagram / YouTube): knowledge + inspiration + discovering the method
NeoFlow Essentiels: structure + practice + feedback + progression
NeoFlow Signature: personalised mentoring + project + professionalisation

Who it's for: advanced beginner to intermediate dancers, training regularly, who want to build confidence, develop their artistic expression, learn to create and better understand their own movement

Duration: 6 weeks
Rhythm: 1 group session per week, 1h30 to 2h
Group size: 8 to 12 dancers maximum
Format: in person or remote depending on availability

Week 1 — Reconnecting with your movement: body awareness, presence and confidence
Week 2 — Technique & intention: turning technique into a tool for expression
Week 3 — Expression: interpretation, musicality, intention and presence
Week 4 — Improvisation: letting your own material emerge
Week 5 — Creation: building a short personal choreographic phrase
Week 6 — Identity & path forward: presenting your work, receiving feedback and defining the next step

What you get: 6 structured sessions, practical exercises, working materials, feedback from Régis, a short personal creation, a progress review and a roadmap to keep going

Please note: NeoFlow Essentiels is a structured artistic practice and mentoring program — it is not an accredited course or a professional certification.

Pricing and upcoming sessions: on request
