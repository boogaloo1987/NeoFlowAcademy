From hip-hop to the contemporary stage

Dancer, choreographer and teacher, Régis Tsoumbou bakana discovered hip-hop in 2008 in Gabon, through the battles and street events of his neighbourhood.

Trained at the École de Danse Afro-Contemporaine Irène Tassembedo (EDIT) in Ouagadougou, then at Université Paris 8, he performs in France and abroad and creates his own pieces with Compagnie DK-BEL.
