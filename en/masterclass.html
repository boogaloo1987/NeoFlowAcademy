Who it's for

For dancers who want a direction

For dancers who train regularly but feel they're missing a method, a direction, or the confidence to go further. No level is required beyond regular practice: the method is designed to build you up, not to filter you out.

The programme

Four chapters

ProposalThe chapters follow a simple thread: support, then intention, then a short creation. The number of chapters, their order and their length are for Régis to set.

  1. Support

    Take the exercise from the free lesson further: where the weight goes, how to feel it and control it.

    To be completedChapter 1 video · length to be defined

  2. Intention

    Turn the same support into an expressive choice: rhythm, quality of movement, presence.

    To be completedChapter 2 video · length to be defined

  3. A short phrase

    Put a few movements together into a short personal phrase, from what has been explored.
