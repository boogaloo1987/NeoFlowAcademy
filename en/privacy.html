2. Data collected

As part of using our automated assistant (WhatsApp agent), we may process:

  • The content of WhatsApp messages (text, transcribed voice notes) sent to our professional WhatsApp Business number.
  • Data necessary for managing calendar and email tasks related to this service (via Google Calendar and Gmail), when these actions are explicitly requested.

3. Purpose of processing

This data is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to requests sent via WhatsApp.

Data may pass through the following services, strictly within the scope of providing the service:

  • An automation host (n8n infrastructure, hosted by OVHcloud, France).
